# How to Use Spndr

A quick guide to get you started. No tech knowledge needed.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Open the app link your friend sent you.
2. Click "Get Started."
3. Enter any email and a password (you do not need to verify the email).
4. You are in.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

After signing up, the app will ask you a few questions:

- **Your name** -- just so the AI knows who to talk to.
- **How often you get money** -- pick Weekly, Monthly, or Flexible.
  - Weekly: You get a fixed amount every week (like a weekly allowance).
  - Monthly: You get money once a month (like a stipend or pocket money).
  - Flexible: Your parents send money whenever you ask.
- **How much you get** -- enter the amount you usually receive.
- **Your current balance** -- how much money you have right now.

Click "Finish" and you will land on your Dashboard.

---

## Step 3: Understanding the Dashboard

Your dashboard shows you everything at a glance:

### The "Can I Survive?" Number (top)
This tells you how much you can spend per day until your next transfer. If this number is low, maybe skip the Starbucks today.

### The Donut Chart
Shows where your money is going. Food? Transport? Entertainment? The bigger the slice, the more you are spending in that category.

### The Bar Chart
Compares your Income vs Expenses for the month. Green bar = money in. Red bar = money out. You want the green bar to be taller.

### The Spending Line
Shows your spending pattern over the last 7 days. If you see a big spike on Friday, you know what happened.

### The Wallet Growth Chart
Shows your balance over the last 30 days. If the line is going up, you are saving. If it is going down, you are spending more than you earn.

### Ask Finance Coach
Click this card to get personalized AI advice about your spending. It will roast you if you are spending too much.

---

## Step 4: Adding Expenses and Income

### The Quick Way (Chat)
Go to "AI Coach" in the bottom navigation. Just type naturally:

- "Spent 200 on lunch"
- "Got 5000 from dad"
- "Bought coffee for 150"

The AI will understand, log it, and probably roast you for it.

### The Manual Way
Go to "Home" and scroll to the transaction form. Fill in:
- Type (Expense or Income)
- Amount
- Description (what was it for)
- Category (the AI will suggest one automatically)

Click "Add" and it is saved.

### The Receipt Way
Below the manual form, there is a "Receipt Scanner." Take a photo of any bill or receipt and the app will read it automatically. Review the details and click "Confirm."

---

## Step 5: Managing Your Budget

Go to "Budgets" from the top navigation.

Here you can:
1. **Set spending limits** for each category (like "Food: 3000 per month").
2. **See your progress** -- a green bar means you are within budget. Yellow means getting close. Red means you went over.

---

## Step 6: Viewing Your History

Go to "History" from the top navigation.

This shows every transaction you have ever logged, grouped by date. You can:
- **Search** for specific transactions.
- **Filter** by category.
- **See the time** each transaction was made.

---

## Step 7: Importing Old Transactions

If you have a bank statement (PDF or CSV) from PhonePe, GPay, or your bank, you can import it:

1. Go to "AI Coach."
2. Look for the "Statement Import" section.
3. Drop your PDF or CSV file.
4. The AI will extract all the transactions.
5. Review them and click "Confirm and Import."

---

## Tips and Tricks

- **Talk to the AI like a friend.** Say "I blew 500 on Zomato" instead of trying to fill out forms.
- **Check the Dashboard daily.** The "Can I Survive?" number is your best friend.
- **Set budgets early.** Even rough limits help you stay aware.
- **The AI is savage on purpose.** It will call you out for overspending. That is the point. Listen to it.
- **Refresh your insights.** Click "Ask Finance Coach" on the dashboard whenever you want a fresh spending summary.

---

## Quick Reference

| Action | Where to go |
|--------|-------------|
| Add an expense | AI Coach (just type it) or Home (manual form) |
| Check your balance | Dashboard (top number) |
| See spending by category | Dashboard (donut chart) |
| Set a budget | Budgets page |
| Find an old transaction | History page |
| Import a bank statement | AI Coach page |
| Scan a receipt | Home page (below the form) |
| Get spending advice | Dashboard (Ask Finance Coach) |
| Log out or switch accounts | Click the user icon (top right) |
| Delete all your data | User icon (top right) then "Delete My Data" |

---

## Need Help?

If something is not working, try:
1. Refreshing the page.
2. Logging out and logging back in.
3. Telling the AI Coach what went wrong -- it might have a fix.

That is it. Start tracking, stay honest, and let the AI keep you in check.
